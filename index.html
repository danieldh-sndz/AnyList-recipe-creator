How it works

  1. Create a recipe with Claude, or Paste one you already have.
  2. Review the fields, then save it to your Library.
  3. Tap Export — the recipe file is saved to Files (usually Downloads).
  4. Tap Open AnyList Import, sign in, choose the file, and tap Import Recipes.
Open AnyList Recipe Import

Full steps are on AnyList's help page. AnyList reads the Paprika recipe format, so that is what this app writes. Your recipes stay on your phone; the only things sent anywhere are your prompt (to Anthropic) and the photo description (to the image service).
